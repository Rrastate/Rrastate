### 👨‍💻 About Me
I'm a **Computer Science & Robotics M.Sc. Student** based in Montpellier, France <!--[cite: 3] -->. 
I specialize in designing and integrating intelligent systems, bridging the gap between embedded hardware, AI, and computer vision <!--[cite: 3] -->.

*   **🎓 Currently:** Enrolled in the Master 2 Informatique & Robotique at the Université de Montpellier <!--[cite: 3] -->.
*   **🔭 Recent Focus:** Developing multimodal interaction systems combining computer vision (YOLOv8), speech recognition (Whisper), and local LLMs (Phi-3) on humanoid platforms like Pepper.
*   **⚙️ Embedded Exploration:** Analyzing memory optimization and comparing hardware abstraction layers (HAL vs. LL drivers) on real-time microcontrollers (STM32).
*   **🌱 Always Learning:** Passionate about continuous skill acquisition, tech watch, and DIY prototyping <!--[cite: 3] -->.
*   **🤝 Community:** I actively volunteer at Repair Café Grand Montpellier, diagnosing and repairing electronics to promote eco-responsibility <!--[cite: 3] -->.
